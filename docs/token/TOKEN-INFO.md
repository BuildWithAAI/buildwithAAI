## Updating this record

Record the full source, chain/program identity, transaction or account evidence, observation time and slot where relevant, verifier, and owner approval. Verify the mint against the platform page independently of its name or ticker. Distinguish absent permissions from missing data, and preserve changes in history. A database estimate is not a substitute for on-chain supply.

Mint and freeze authorities are separate roles; extensions may introduce additional controls. No single revoked role proves a token safe. [Solana authority documentation](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/set-authority).

This file does not set supply, choose allocations, create a token, alter an authority, or attest to behavior not actually checked. Platform defaults must be verified at the time of the real launch rather than guessed here.
