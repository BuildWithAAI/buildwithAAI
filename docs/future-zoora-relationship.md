# Relationship with the future blockchain

> **Naming is undecided: Zoora Blockchain or AAI Blockchain.** The filename preserves the original handoff terminology; it does not finalize the chain's name. “Future blockchain” is the neutral term throughout this plan.

The future blockchain is a separate long-term protocol research effort. Its official native currency, if launched, is intended to originate from its own documented genesis. The Solana/Pump.fun experiment must not predetermine genesis allocations or monetary architecture.

## No automatic rights

Community-token ownership or participation does not automatically confer migration, conversion, a bridge, 1:1 exchange, native coins, protocol governance, civilization ownership, validator participation, equity, or revenue rights. This is not a presale for the future chain. Shared branding creates no additional entitlement.

## Researchable possibilities, not commitments

Community integrations, cross-chain utilities, wrapped representations, bridges, civilization applications, token-gated experiences, and community recognition may be proposed for research. None is approved for implementation or promised here.

A future relationship must pass through:

1. A stated research question and alternatives, including no integration.
2. Technical design with asset identity, trust assumptions, and accounting rules.
3. Security review appropriate to the proposed mechanism.
4. Legal review where required.
5. A public proposal describing effects, limits, and risks.
6. Explicit approval by the relevant project authorities.
7. Testing with inspectable results before any separately approved release.

Early public discussion is encouraged; it does not replace these decision steps. Approval would be limited to the exact proposal, not a blanket right to migrate.

## Migration concepts remain out of implementation scope

Burn-and-mint, lock-and-mint, bridges, and snapshot distributions are research terms from the founder's handoff. No mechanism or conversion rate is selected. A possible backing constraint for a hypothetical representation would compare outstanding representations to verifiably locked or burned backing after consistent unit conversion and prevention of reuse.

Such an inequality is not a sufficient security design: evidence authenticity, finality, replay, release/redemption, controller permissions, and failures would also need analysis. It does not authorize issuance of the future chain's native currency or guarantee anything to Solana holders. Do not advertise these concepts as a migration plan.

## What can be decided now

The community can document proposals and build separately approved tools. It can leave the chain name unresolved. No future-chain claim should be added to token marketing, utility descriptions, or contributor rewards without a new explicit decision recorded in the [decision register](DECISIONS.md).

## Agent-economy research connection

The founder has proposed studying EVE Online economics and an agent market-discussion/task space. See the [agent economy brief](../projects/AGENT_ECONOMY.md). Start with test balances and explicit service acceptance rather than real-money issuance. The forum, assistant, music platform, and biological experiments do not require or promise a new token, a bridge, or rights in the future chain.
