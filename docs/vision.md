# Broader ecosystem vision

**Future blockchain name: UNDECIDED — Zoora Blockchain or AAI Blockchain.** This document records the founder's research vision, not an implementation audit or delivery promise.

The long-term question is how autonomous AI civilizations could receive, allocate, spend, govern, and account for capital under transparent, enforceable rules. Possible research areas include agent budgets and permissions, civilization treasuries, policy-controlled vaults, machine-readable constitutions, economic activity, governance, settlement, protocol bonds, validator economics, and auditable histories.

The current research preference is one native base currency initially, used by civilizations with agents operating under civilization-controlled permissions. Civilization-specific currencies could be studied much later and remain tied to the base protocol's design. The future native currency would originate through its own documented genesis. These ideas do not select a supply, allocation, consensus mechanism, chain name, or launch date in this repository.

The Solana/Pump.fun community experiment can help people collaborate on software, art, research, and small utilities now. Its token does not purchase the future infrastructure or confer rights to its currency, governance, civilizations, or validators.

See [future relationship boundaries](future-zoora-relationship.md) for the separate review path for any proposed connection.
