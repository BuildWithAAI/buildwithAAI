# Creator-fee funding and community benefit

**Updated September 22, 2026. Status: founder intent recorded; allocation, account operation, and distributions remain PROPOSED.**

The founder intends to use creator fees allocated to AAI for development, marketing, and relevant DEX-related costs. The later clarification adds a proposal to fund a dedicated trading account from creator fees, with technology such as an XAUUSD system considered only if research and testing support it. The funding source is creator fees; the earlier assumption of an account funded solely from unrelated personal savings is superseded.

This describes intended use. It is not evidence that fees have been routed, an account has been funded, a bot is profitable, or a distribution has occurred. Personal callout rewards or screenshots are not proof of AAI project income.

## Proposed flow

Record actual creator-fee receipts → apply an approved allocation → fund the dedicated account → operate only under a separately accepted strategy and control policy → reconcile results and costs → approve any withdrawal or distribution.

The amount or percentage allocated to trading is undecided. Existing development/marketing/DEX uses remain in the plan; this proposal does not allocate all creator fees to trading. Any allocated capital can be lost.

## Possible uses of resulting profits

- Further software development and infrastructure.
- Developer thank-you gifts or community/contributor rewards.
- Separately contracted work or bounties when explicitly funded and agreed.
- Disclosed purchases of the community token at prevailing market prices, if separately reviewed and approved.

The founder wants giving back to become a recurring practice if resources and results permit it. No schedule, minimum amount, holder entitlement, or guaranteed return is offered. A discretionary gift is different from promised compensation. A buyback does not guarantee increased or maintained token price and must not be presented as guaranteed chart support.

For any proposed token purchase, record its purpose, approved budget, controller, execution records, and treatment of acquired tokens. Holding, redistributing, and burning tokens are distinct decisions; none is chosen by this document.

## Proposed ledger

Track date, transaction reference, category, asset/currency, amount, controller, purpose, supporting evidence, and authorization. Preserve original asset quantities and any valuation rate/time. Reconcile exchange statements and on-chain transfers; a public wallet alone may not show an exchange-account loss or liability.

Separate creator-fee receipts, account deposits/withdrawals, realized trading gains/losses, unrealized position changes, commissions/funding/data/compute costs, development expenses, gifts, contracted compensation, and token purchases. **Deposits and transfers are not trading profit.** Reconcile opening equity plus external deposits minus withdrawals plus net trading result to closing equity, with valuation changes and unresolved differences explicitly explained.

Publish project-controlled wallets and any real restrictions in the [wallet register](token/WALLETS.md) only after verification. Redact secrets and recipients' private information while preserving appropriately shareable accounting evidence. Reporting templates are not records of actual receipts.

This account proposal does not authorize pooled public deposits, trade execution, a distribution contract, or a token-economics change. It does not alter the future blockchain's separate genesis or grant native-coin, revenue, or governance rights.
