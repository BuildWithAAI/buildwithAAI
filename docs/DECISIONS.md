# Decision and commitment register

**Read this before treating any roadmap item as official.** The future blockchain name is undecided: **Zoora Blockchain or AAI Blockchain**. No native ticker, genesis allocation, or relationship is selected by this draft.

## Commitment categories

| Category | Meaning | Items in this plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| APPROVED | Explicit founder direction, within its stated scope | Community development; separation from the future chain; genesis-origin intent for its native coin; truthful status; no secret collection or automatic trading; owner approval for protected changes |
| IDEA | Suggested product or design, not accepted for implementation | Dashboard, bots, meme tools, profiles, reputation, token-gated features, AAI service payments |
| RESEARCH | An evidence question, not a product outcome | Launch behavior, jellyfish-inspired models, gold strategy evaluation, data definitions, future interoperability questions |
| FUTURE | Deferred possibility with prerequisites and no commitment | Reviewed bounties, new chain infrastructure, advanced integrations, any separately approved token launch |
| NOT PROMISED | Entitlements or outcomes the project does not offer | Migration, bridge, 1:1 conversion, native coins, governance/validator/revenue rights, equity, ownership, utility, profits, launch dates |

Research being permitted does not mean anyone has performed it. Approval to draft a plan does not approve every feature, budget, or launch described in it.

## Open decisions

Official community-token launch name/ticker; tokenomics; mint and launch date; wallets and allocations; licenses; maintainer additions; compensation and bounties; operational budgets; formal reporting cadence; actual platform settings; and the future chain name. Existing AAI branding is not a decision on any of these.

The founder's stated fee-use intent is retained in [FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md); no percentages, spending approvals, or community treasury are inferred.

## Recording later decisions

For each material decision, add ID, date, exact scope, author/maintainer, evidence links, alternatives, risks, token impact, owner decision, additional review where needed, and superseded decision IDs. Preserve rejected and deferred reasoning. A proposal label or merge event alone must not be used as evidence of a broader approval.
