# Development bounties — future proposal

**FUTURE / DEFERRED. No bounty is offered or funded by this repository plan.** A task, contribution, merged PR or token holding does not create a right to payment.

Before any real-money bounty opens, the owner must approve its scope, budget, payment source and accountable controller; acceptance criteria; eligibility and conflicts; reviewer; licensing terms; dispute/cancellation process; payment conditions; and required review of the arrangement. Do not request wallet secrets or unnecessary personal information.

| Bounty status | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed | Scope, objective acceptance tests, proposed amount/asset and owner decision pending |
| Funded | Evidence that the approved funds are available, their controller and restrictions; never describe unverified funds as locked |
| Claimed | Named contributor accepts the published terms; reviewer confirms the claim |
| Submitted | Reviewable artifact and evidence supplied |
| Under review | Reviewer records checks and any required changes |
| Accepted | Acceptance decision identifies the exact deliverable and payment obligation under the agreed terms |
| Paid | Actual payment evidence and date, with appropriate privacy treatment |

Record rejection, cancellation or dispute outcomes explicitly; do not call them paid. Never automate payouts merely because a PR merged. Selecting an escrow or custody design would be a new proposal, not part of this draft. [Idea brief](../../proposals/ideas/AAI-P-0006-bounties.md).
