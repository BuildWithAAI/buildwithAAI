## Problem

Who has the problem? Include evidence where available.

## Proposed solution and smallest useful output

Describe scope, exclusions, alternatives and why this helps. Distinguish existing work from the new proposal.

## Technical requirements and dependencies

Identify platforms, sources, permissions, data terms, staffing and estimated operating costs. Unknown is an acceptable answer. Do not include secrets.

## Risks and security considerations

Cover privacy, abuse, data errors, outages and wallet interaction where relevant. Does this affect supply, minting, authorities, treasury, fees, migration, governance or project-controlled wallets? Do not collect wallet secrets, implement unapproved automatic trading, or imply future-chain rights. Label future execution questions separately from current offline/paper work.

## Acceptance criteria and validation

List observable outcomes and meaningful checks. Separate proposed tests from tests already run. Include rollback/recovery needs where applicable.

## Community feedback

Link discussion and summarize agreements, objections and unresolved questions without claiming consensus from likes or holdings.

## Decision

Pending. Record decision maker, date, scope, reasoning, conditions, evidence and additional review if required. Approval of an idea is not funding, deployment or an entitlement to future utility.
