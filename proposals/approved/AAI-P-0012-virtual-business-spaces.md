## Problem and smallest useful result

Businesses need defined digital spaces and clear terms for access, rights, costs and termination. Agents should prepare compliant drafts and coordinate recorded lifecycle decisions while an external deterministic policy prevents unauthorized terms or fund operations.

Build a small offline leasing laboratory alongside the current community/economy work. Its first output is a versioned contract schema/validator, simulated listing/lease lifecycle, exact separate rent/fee accounting and replayable adverse scenarios. All simulated acceptance, payments and allocations are labeled SYNTHETIC with no monetary value.

## Founder acceptance and conditions

The founder stated: “Let’s make this known and a project for the agents to develop” and required “a strict rule set that the contracts must go by.” The earlier fee discussion accepted the direction of funding development and strengthening the economy through AAI treasury accumulation, burns and liquidity support.

This records approval to publish the project and develop its specification/simulation phase. The 10%-of-rent service fee and 50/30/20 net-fee allocation are proposed experiment values, not a reviewed production tariff. Actual templates/jurisdiction, mint/payment destinations, amounts/splits, wallet execution, LP controls, hosting, legal review and public launch remain separately scoped. Existing agent-access bond policies and the future chain's native currency are unchanged.

## Requirements, risks and acceptance

The [project brief](../../projects/VIRTUAL_BUSINESS_SPACES.md) defines dependencies, allocation denominators, proposed scenarios and task completion evidence. The [mandatory rules](../../projects/virtual-business-spaces/CONTRACT_RULES.md) control clauses, parties/authority, availability, disclosed fees, agreement-specific consent, direct payments, earning/reserves, public reporting and protected review.

Proposed tests cover double-booking, conflicting renewal, expired/forged/replayed consent, altered terms, stale quotes, prompt injection, rent/fee misattribution, repeated receipts, refund exposure, operating coverage, concentration, paired liquidity asset shortage, outage/restart and deterministic replay. None has run for this new project yet.

No autonomous legal advice, customer funds custody, real signer, burn/LP execution, price-stability guarantee, holder profit entitlement or native-chain change is approved by this first phase. Legal enforceability and paid launch require separate review. No contributor compensation or community consensus is invented.
