## Problem and benefit

Some community features might benefit from verifiable participation, but the need for an on-chain component is unproven.

## Proposed solution

Compare wallet profiles, holder badges, achievements, reputation, collectibles, token-gated access and non-binding community feedback experiments. Select at most one synthetic prototype to propose.

## Technical requirements and dependencies

Clear user benefit, privacy model, consent, permission design, data retention, anti-replay design for any future signature flow and separate program review if ever needed.

## Risks and security considerations

Phishing, replay, hidden permissions, exclusion, tracking, Sybil abuse and confusing an experimental badge or poll with protocol rights. No mainnet signing or transfers in this proposal.

## Acceptance criteria for the proposal

- Compare a wallet-free option and explain why a wallet might help.
- Describe the exact information shown before any proposed wallet interaction.
- State that experiments confer no governance, native coins, conversion, revenue or ownership in the future chain.
