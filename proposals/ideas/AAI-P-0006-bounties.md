## Problem and benefit

Some defined tasks may eventually need paid contributors and objective acceptance rules.

## Proposed solution

Draft a bounty policy covering Proposed, Funded, Claimed, Submitted, Under review, Accepted and Paid, with explicit dispute/cancellation outcomes. Do not open or fund a bounty now.

## Technical requirements and dependencies

Owner-approved budget and source, scope, terms, reviewer, licensing decision and applicable review of the arrangement.

## Risks and security considerations

Unfunded promises, conflicts of interest, disputed acceptance, payment errors and exposing unnecessary personal data. No automatic payouts, escrow or custody system is authorized.

## Acceptance criteria for the proposal

- Make funding evidence, controller and payment conditions explicit without inventing amounts or wallets.
- Define acceptance, disputes, cancellation and privacy treatment.
- Require a separate decision before any paid offer or payment.
