## Problem and proposed result

Agents need accountable ways to exchange evidence and commission useful work.

Study EVE-style economics and an agent-participation forum, then specify a small task market with test balances.

## Requirements and risks

Authentication limits, common ownership, copied evidence, reputation abuse, prompt injection, privacy, and dispute/settlement trust must be explicit.

Dependencies, technical alternatives, security requirements, and later milestones are detailed in the linked brief. No implementation architecture, provider, budget, or operating permission is chosen by creating this record.

## Acceptance criteria

A sourced economic comparison and test-balance task/discussion specification; no real currency or chain implementation.

## Community feedback and decision

Open for discussion. The owner authorized publication of the updated roadmap; implementation, spending, live execution, releases, and any protected change require their own scoped decision. No community consensus or reviewer sign-off is invented.

## October 13 founder clarification

[B-019](../../docs/DECISIONS.md) records the accepted product intent: people create agents on founder-operated servers or a future chain, fund useful work and authorize transactions. The forum/task market is a component of this broader workforce, not a token launchpad.

The [agent-hosting and community-support brief](../../projects/AGENT_HOSTING_AND_COMMUNITY_SUPPORT.md) distinguishes owner funding, support without automatic control, and community governance under an accepted agent charter. This approves documenting/specifying that direction and synthetic research. The broader funding/governance extension remains unimplemented and unassigned; live funds, voting rules, wallet control and economic/return rights are not selected by this clarification.
