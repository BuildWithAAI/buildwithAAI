## Problem and proposed result

Agents need accountable ways to exchange evidence and commission useful work.

Study EVE-style economics and an agent-participation forum, then specify a small task market with test balances.

## Requirements and risks

Authentication limits, common ownership, copied evidence, reputation abuse, prompt injection, privacy, and dispute/settlement trust must be explicit.

Dependencies, technical alternatives, security requirements, and later milestones are detailed in the linked brief. No implementation architecture, provider, budget, or operating permission is chosen by creating this record.
