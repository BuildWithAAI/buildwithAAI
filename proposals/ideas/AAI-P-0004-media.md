## Problem and benefit

Contributors need reusable assets that are fun and consistent without promising financial outcomes.

## Proposed solution

Create proposed branding templates, memes, GIFs, social graphics, stickers and X assets. A simple meme generator may be proposed after the asset pack is reviewed.

## Technical requirements and dependencies

Founder brand review, original or licensed assets, source files, font terms and accessibility requirements.

## Risks and security considerations

Unlicensed content, impersonation, invented mint links and imagery that implies guaranteed returns or future-chain entitlements.

## Acceptance criteria for the proposal

- Deliver editable samples with provenance and usage terms.
- Check small-screen readability and avoid real-looking placeholder mint addresses.
- Keep future blockchain naming undecided; AAI community branding does not settle it.

## Community feedback

Not collected in this draft. Link the discussion and preserve objections and alternatives when available.

## Decision

Pending. This is an idea record, not implementation, funding, launch or deployment approval. Any substantive architecture change must be shown to the owner first. The future blockchain name is undecided between Zoora Blockchain and AAI Blockchain; this Solana experiment creates no rights to that future network or its native currency.
