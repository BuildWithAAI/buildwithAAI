## Problem and proposed result

Musicians need clear ways to invite open verses, collaborate across genres, and release permitted versions with credit.

Specify original uploads, invitations, submissions, approval, credits/agreed splits, remix lineage, and labeled AI collaboration.

## Requirements and risks

Recording/composition/sample rights, consent, onward remixes, external distribution, takedowns, and voice/likeness permissions require review.

Dependencies, technical alternatives, security requirements, and later milestones are detailed in the linked brief. No implementation architecture, provider, budget, or operating permission is chosen by creating this record.

## Acceptance criteria

An authorized-material demonstration flow and rights specification; no automatic artist signing, label launch, or music coin.
