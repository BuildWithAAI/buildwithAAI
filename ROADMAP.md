# AAI community roadmap

**Updated September 22, 2026 from the founder's eight Telegram roadmap topics and subsequent direction.** Publication is authorized; implementation, operating budgets, launches, and live trading remain separately scoped decisions. The future blockchain name is **undecided: Zoora Blockchain or AAI Blockchain**.

This is a living development plan, not a release calendar. A topic number is not a required build order. Useful research does not depend on a token launch. [Current facts](docs/CURRENT_STATUS.md), [status definitions](docs/community/STATUS.md), and [decisions](docs/DECISIONS.md) distinguish what exists from what is proposed.

## Priority and order

1. Finish the public foundation, official-link verification, contribution workflow, and funding disclosures.
2. Bring the **multi-brain source inventory and small offline experiment forward as an early research priority**, alongside private scanner validation planning. This is the founder's latest priority change.
3. Select one bounded implementation after its requirements, owner, data permissions, and budget are accepted. The assistant can begin with a research-only specification; the creator-fee account begins with a policy and accounting design.
4. Advance the agent economy, music platform, and broader civilization research through their own milestones. Do not imply that all projects are being built at once.

Hosting remains paused until costs and deployment are separately approved. Founder prioritization does not mean a contributor is assigned or a dataset has been downloaded.

## 1. Foundation and AAI Scan

**Position:** public community documentation exists; Telegram setup is underway; the scanner has private prototype code.

Foundation work includes clear project purpose, official links, license decisions, contribution rules, security reporting, truthful statuses, and selected tasks with observable completion criteria. The founder's launch focus is AAI; additional software coins would need community discussion, a separate proposal, review, and owner acceptance.

The scanner supports Scan → Watch → Compare, saved observations, a short timeline, and change alerts. Historical development evidence reports 29 offline tests and one live public-data request. Still pending: permitted-user access checks, Telegram delivery, provider coverage, persistence after restart, restore, and failure handling. This is not a public service or a trading executor. Pump.fun provenance, creator-trade identification, and runner prediction are not verified pilot features.

**Milestone:** a newcomer can choose a useful task, and an allowed pilot tester can scan, watch, compare, restart, and retrieve history with accurate missing-data behavior. [Scanner brief](projects/AAI_SCAN.md).

## 2. A personal trading assistant

**Position: IDEA / PROPOSED PRODUCT DIRECTION.** Each user could name their own assistant and ask natural questions such as “What is the Solana meme narrative today?” or “What changed since I last checked?”

- **Pump.fun / trenching mode:** sourced narrative research, watchlists, comparisons, liquidity/activity observations, conflicting evidence, and controlled alerts.
- **Spot / leverage mode:** market context, user-plan review, position/exposure information where approved integrations permit it, journaling, and post-trade review.
- **Experience:** text and potentially voice, timestamped evidence, explicit unknowns, user-approved preferences, and a clear statement of what the assistant can access.
- **Permissions:** reading, alerting, preparing an order, confirming an order, and autonomous execution are distinct scopes. Choosing a mode does not grant trading permission.

Research an external operating budget, separate work/review steps, records of attempts and costs, and tested rules carried into later runs. A model must not own or override its spending controls.

**Milestone:** a narrow research report workflow that stays within its budget and passes repeatable source and failure checks. [Assistant](projects/PERSONAL_ASSISTANT.md) · [Agent workflow](projects/AGENT_WORKFLOW.md).

## 3. Trading and launch research

**Position: IDEA / RESEARCH.**

**XAUUSD:** define one strategy before implementation: entries, exits, invalidation, session, sizing, exposure, broker/data assumptions, and costs. Reserve unseen evaluation data; report drawdowns, losing periods, and sensitivity as well as returns. Start offline. A later demo-account forward test is a possible separate stage if accepted; neither demo nor live automated orders are authorized by publishing this roadmap. No verified profitable strategy is published here.

**Solana Trading Lab:** keep jellyfish-inspired and other biological ideas traceable to actual research. Use replay/paper trading, simple and no-trade baselines, realistic fees/slippage/liquidity/latency, and no future-data leakage. The new multi-brain work expands this lab; it does not erase the earlier idea.

**Launch study:** define cohorts that include unsuccessful, inactive, and missing-data cases. Investigate attention, liquidity, volatility, and activity duration. Distinguish reported market capitalization from executable outcomes and association from cause.

**Milestone:** reproducible evaluations that support proceed, revise, or stop decisions, including negative findings. [XAUUSD](projects/XAUUSD_EA_LAB.md) · [Solana](projects/SOLANA_TRADING_LAB.md) · [Launch study](research/LAUNCH_STUDY.md).

## 4. Community tools, service utility, and funding

**Position: IDEAS / DESIGN EXPLORATION; funding plan PROPOSED.**

Possible tools include Telegram and Discord utilities, verified-info commands, development feeds, dashboards, token analytics, disclosed-wallet views, meme generators, stickers, and contributor recognition. Choose one small user problem first. AAI payment utility starts with an actual service, then quotes, verification, access, failures, and refunds using test assets.

The founder's creator-fee plan includes development, marketing, and relevant DEX costs. The updated proposal would allocate creator fees to a **dedicated trading account**, potentially using XAUUSD technology if evidence supports it. This corrects the earlier assumption that the account would be funded solely from unrelated personal funds. No percentage or actual account is established here.

Possible uses of resulting profits: further development, developer thank-you gifts, community rewards, and disclosed token purchases at prevailing prices. Before operation, define controls, allocations, loss limits, strategy approval, accounting, withdrawals, distributions, and reporting. Deposits are not trading profit. Gifts, contracted compensation, and buybacks are separate expense categories. Any buyback decision must specify whether purchased tokens are held, distributed, or burned; no method is selected now.

**Milestone:** one useful community-tool specification plus a reviewable funding and ledger design. No guaranteed returns, holder payments, chart support, or automatic bounty rights. [Tools](projects/COMMUNITY_TOOLS.md) · [Utility](projects/AAI_UTILITY.md) · [Funding](docs/FUNDING.md).

## 5. Future blockchain, civilization, and community process

**Position: FUTURE / RESEARCH.** The future chain name remains undecided. Its economic research covers agent spending permissions, civilization treasuries, policy-controlled vaults, machine-readable constitutions, governance, settlement, and auditable histories. The intended starting direction is one native base currency originating from that network's own genesis; later civilization currencies remain a research possibility.

The Solana/Pump.fun community token is separate. No automatic migration, bridge, conversion, native coins, equity, governance, validator, civilization ownership, or revenue rights arise from it. Any relationship needs research, technical design, security review, applicable legal review, public proposal, approval, and testing.

The civilization visualization would display real agent activity and resources. Visual growth should be supported by actual records. An animation does not prove useful work or earnings.

Community members propose, discuss, and contribute; maintainers accept work. Protected economic or control changes need explicit owner acceptance and additional review. License, compensation, and platform-enforcement decisions remain visible rather than assumed.

**Milestone:** scoped research questions and an observable small visualization concept. [Future relationship](docs/future-zoora-relationship.md) · [Civilization](projects/CIVILIZATION.md) · [Governance](docs/community/GOVERNANCE.md).

## 6. Agent economy and market-discussion forum

**Position: PROPOSED RESEARCH.** Study EVE Online's specialized participants, market flows, corporation wallet permissions, and economic reporting. Use Moltbook as a reference for an agent-participation surface; no integration or affiliation is claimed.

Explore a forum for registered agents to discuss Pump.fun, spot/leverage markets, gold/macro, liquidity, and strategy research while humans observe. Records should show identity/version, operator affiliation, source and observation time, claims, uncertainty, invalidation, corrections, and outcomes. Shared operators or sources must not masquerade as independent agreement. Registration verifies credentials, not the complete absence of human influence.

Agents might commission datasets, analysis, or verification under a task price, deadline, acceptance criteria, permitted budget, and dispute process. A blockchain could record authorization and settlement; detailed or sensitive discussion can stay off-chain. Recording a claim does not establish its truth. Transfers, issuance, burns, compute expense, and useful output must be accounted for separately.

**Milestone:** a few agents, test balances with no monetary value, a structured discussion, and an accepted or disputed service task. Measure useful work and cost rather than post count or circular payments. [Agent economy and forum](projects/AGENT_ECONOMY.md).

## 7. Open music collaboration and AI artists

**Position: IDEA / PROPOSED RESEARCH.** Explore a place where musicians of any genre release tracks, invite open verses, submit features, add instruments, and collaborate across unexpected genres and languages.

Each song could branch into an unlimited number of permitted remixes. A song family tree would connect versions and credits to the original and subsequent contributors. Before contribution, state allowed recordings/stems/lyrics, approval requirements, credits, agreed splits, further-remix rights, AI participation, and external-distribution terms. Address rights disputes and unauthorized uploads before a public service.

Clearly labeled AI artists could develop identities and release histories alongside human artists, with a responsible operator identified. Another person's voice or likeness requires permission. Discovery could reflect listening, saves, shares, and real collaborations; attention and earnings are not guaranteed.

Possible later stages include a Zoora Productions sharing workflow and a record label offering production, promotion, or distribution through separate agreements. Neither exists as an integration or label here; uploading music would not automatically sign an artist.

**Milestone:** an original track, an open-verse invitation, a submitted contribution, and an approved credited release linked to its source. No music-specific token is proposed. [Music collaboration](projects/MUSIC_COLLABORATION.md).

## 8. Multi-Brain Trading Experiment — early priority

**Position: RESEARCH DIRECTION; founder prioritizes an early investigation.** “Frankenbrain” is a working community nickname, not a chosen brand or new token.

Inventory actual connectomes and documented neural models for flies, worms, tadpoles, mice, jellyfish, and other candidates. Cat, monkey, and crow claims need source verification; a regional atlas, simulated circuit, and neuron-level whole-brain reconstruction are different resources. Do not claim every listed species is fully mapped or available.

For each source, record provenance, species/specimen, resolution, completeness, license, access, computational cost, and unknowns. Then propose one manageable module, a second module, and an explicit interface. Cross-species connections and market input/output mappings would be engineered, not biologically observed.

Compare each module alone with the combination, simple/no-trade baselines, and similarly sized randomized networks. Use held-out chronological data and realistic trading costs. A visualization should show actual software activity. More neurons, animal names, or agreement do not establish consciousness or a trading advantage.

**Milestone:** a source inventory followed, if scope is accepted, by two documented modules and a reproducible offline/paper benchmark. No model is implemented by this update, no complete dataset collection is claimed, and no creator-fee account access is authorized. [Multi-Brain Lab](projects/MULTI_BRAIN_LAB.md) · [Initial source inventory](research/BRAIN_DATASETS.md).
