# Project boundaries

**Founder-directed boundaries; explanatory documentation, not a legal opinion.**

Build With AAI is a Solana/Pump.fun community experiment associated with the broader Zoora ecosystem. The future standalone blockchain name is **undecided: Zoora Blockchain or AAI Blockchain**. Neither name is finalized.

## Separate assets and decisions

The November 9, 2026 agent-economy plan removes any token. Earlier references to a community token are historical. They are not a presale, equity in Zoora Technologies, or ownership of Zoora or its protocol. v1 does not include a native coin, a conversion, a bridge, or an allocation right.

Participation does not create rights to migration, governance, validator participation, revenue, or contributor payments. No future utility, availability, price outcome, profit, or return is guaranteed.

The community experiment must not determine the future chain's genesis allocations or tokenomics. Research on future relationships is not approval to implement them. Naming that chain Zoora or AAI does not alter these boundaries.

## Experimental work and current control

Ideas, research, prototypes, tests, and releases are labeled separately. A roadmap is not a delivery guarantee. Historical observations and simulations do not establish future trading outcomes. This repository does not certify that a token is safe, approved by a regulator, or legally classified in a particular way.

No mint, launch date, supply, allocation, or authority state is recorded in this revision. The [token record](docs/token/TOKEN-INFO.md) is an empty template. Missing values are **TBD — DO NOT GUESS**. v1 payments, when built, are classic SPL USDC from the user's own wallet. Zoora does not hold those funds.

Development invites community participation. Final acceptance remains with owner/maintainers; it is not fully decentralized governance. The founder's fee-funding intent is in [FUNDING.md](docs/FUNDING.md). No community treasury or holder distribution is established here.

Any future chain relationship requires research, technical design, security review, legal review where required, a public proposal, approval, and testing.
