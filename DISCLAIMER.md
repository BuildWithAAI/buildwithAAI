# Project boundaries

**Founder-directed boundaries; explanatory documentation, not a legal opinion.**

Build With AAI is a Solana/Pump.fun community experiment associated with the broader Zoora ecosystem. The future standalone blockchain name is **undecided: Zoora Blockchain or AAI Blockchain**. Neither name is finalized.

## Separate assets and decisions

The community token is not that future chain's native currency, a presale for it, equity in Zoora Technologies, or ownership of Zoora or its protocol. The future native currency, if launched, is intended to originate through the future network's documented genesis process.

Participation or token ownership does not automatically create rights to migration, conversion, 1:1 exchange, a bridge, native coins, governance, validator participation, civilization ownership, revenue, or contributor payments. No future utility, availability, token-price outcome, profit, or return is guaranteed.

The community experiment must not determine the future chain's genesis allocations or tokenomics. Research on future relationships is not approval to implement them. Naming that chain Zoora or AAI does not alter these boundaries.

## Experimental work and current control

Ideas, research, prototypes, tests, and releases are labeled separately. A roadmap is not a delivery guarantee. Historical observations and simulations do not establish future trading outcomes. This repository does not certify that a token is safe, approved by a regulator, or legally classified in a particular way.

No verified community-token mint, launch date, supply, allocation, or authority state is recorded in this revision. Use the [canonical token record](docs/token/TOKEN-INFO.md); missing values are **TBD — DO NOT GUESS**.

Development invites community participation. Final acceptance remains with owner/maintainers; it is not fully decentralized governance. The founder's fee-funding intent is in [FUNDING.md](docs/FUNDING.md). No community treasury or holder distribution is established here.

Any future chain relationship requires research, technical design, security review, legal review where required, a public proposal, approval, and testing.

## New roadmap ideas

The creator-fee trading account, possible gifts/rewards/buybacks, personal assistant, multi-brain experiment, agent economy/forum, music collaboration, AI artists, potential label, and civilization display are explicitly scoped proposals or research. Publishing them does not fund an account, authorize trading, establish distributions, select a license, certify an animal simulation, or promise a product or profitable result.

The founder's AAI-only current launch focus is an intention with a separately reviewed software-coin exception. It neither announces another token nor cancels the distinction between the community token and any future genesis-native currency.
