# Launch behavior and volatility study

**Status:** proposed research protocol; no dataset or results are supplied by this document.

## Question

Which observable characteristics are associated with different launch outcomes, and which patterns remain useful when tested on a later period? The founder's interest includes large runners, sustained activity, and volatility on Solana / Pump.fun.

Define the question before choosing examples. This study cannot promise a perfect launch or establish that following a pattern will produce a particular market capitalization.

## Proposed protocol

1. Define a date range, venue, mint-creation criteria, and observation windows. Verify launch provenance from appropriate records rather than a token name or address suffix.
2. Include failed launches, inactive tokens, unindexed cases, and incomplete records. Track exclusions and gaps explicitly.
3. Define outcomes separately: peak reported market cap, time above a threshold, reported liquidity, drawdown, activity duration, and simulated executable returns after costs. Market cap is not money invested or available exit liquidity.
4. Record features only from information available at the stated decision time. Specify whether counts refer to transactions, token accounts, wallets, or a documented estimate of participants.
5. Document how paid promotion, artificial activity, suspected common control, and provider coverage might distort interpretation. Treat unverified attribution as uncertain.
6. Keep a later cohort out of model selection. Report sample sizes, missingness, uncertainty, and performance after realistic costs.
7. Publish source definitions, collection code where permitted, and reproducible summaries, including negative findings.

Observed associations do not by themselves establish causes. Attention, price, liquidity, and trading activity can influence each other.

## What AAI Scan contributes

The private pilot can collect forward observations for selected watches. It is not a platform-wide historical dataset and does not yet verify Pump.fun launch origin. A hand-picked watch list cannot establish overall launch success rates.

## First community output

A cohort specification and data-source inventory with coverage, retention, reuse terms, and estimated collection costs. Do not spend on data before the maintainer approves the scope and budget.
