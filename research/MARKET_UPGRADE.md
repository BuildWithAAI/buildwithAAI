## What the book actually is

- Market cap and FDV about $2.9K.
- Liquidity about $4.6K, roughly 23 SOL in the pool.
- 24-hour volume $0.
- About 271 holders. No one holds a real position.
- Seven months old. LP 100% locked. Mint and freeze disabled.
- Historical bundled buys about 27%. Security score about 50.

The chart is chopped because the pool is a constant-product book with almost no depth. A 1 SOL buy against 23 SOL moves the price about 4%. A 5 SOL buy moves it about 18%. With no flow, the chart is the residue of old thin trades. That is not stealth. It is an empty book.

## What not to do

Do not wash volume. Do not paint the chart. Do not coordinate a pump. Do not hide supply. Those make the chart look busy and make the token worse. LP is already locked and mint is already off. Leave both.

## Real upgrades

**1. Depth before narrative.**
The only thing that cleans a PumpSwap chart is more SOL in the pool, left there.

| SOL in pool | 1 SOL slippage | 5 SOL slippage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 23 | 4.2% | 18.0% |
| 50 | 2.0% | 9.1% |
| 100 | 1.0% | 4.8% |
| 250 | 0.4% | 2.0% |

Add depth in one disclosed transaction. Do not drip it in a way that looks like stealth bids. Lock the added LP the same way the current LP is locked.

**2. Burn from fees, not from a press release.**
A supply burn with no demand is cosmetic. The burn that matters is a buyback funded only by agent-economy fees: hire fees, practice settlement, and later real job fees. Split that fee. Half buys AAI and burns it. Half stays as operating SOL. If fees are zero, the burn is zero. Publish the burn transaction. Do not burn treasury tokens to fake scarcity.

**3. Inventory, not holders.**
271 holders with dust is not a market. Gate useful agent access on a time-weighted hold: days held times a square-root of size, capped at 90 days. A wallet that holds a real slice for a month can hire a specialist. A fresh dust wallet cannot. That creates a reason to hold without promising a price.

**4. One demand path.**
The agent economy has to pay for something in AAI or route a fee into the pool. Until a hire, a practice settlement, or a job fee touches this pool, the chart will stay chopped no matter the narrative.

## Order

1. Disclose and deepen the pool. Lock the new LP.
2. Wire agent fees to a public buyback-burn. Zero fees means zero burn.
3. Gate specialist hire on the inventory score.
4. Only then talk about the chart.

This is not a price forecast. It is the minimum structure that makes a clean book possible.
