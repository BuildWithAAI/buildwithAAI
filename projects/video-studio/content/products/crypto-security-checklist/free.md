## Your seed phrase {#free-seed}

- [ ] **Type it into one place only: the wallet itself,** during a recovery you started. No website, app, form, chat or AI tool ever needs it. [@ledger-phishing; @ethereum-security]
- [ ] **Delete every photo, screenshot, note and cloud copy.** Malware in official app stores has read recovery phrases from photo galleries. If a copy existed, move to a new wallet. [@kaspersky-sparkcat-2025]
- [ ] **Write it by hand on paper or metal,** test the backup, and keep copies in two safe places.
- [ ] **Anyone who asks for it is a thief,** even a printed letter on a wallet maker's letterhead. [@theblock-ledger-letters-2025]

## Accounts and logins {#free-accounts}

- [ ] **Use a security key or passkey** on your email and exchanges. Text-message codes are the weakest option. [@cisa-mfa-2022; @nist-800-63b-4]
- [ ] **Ask your carrier for SIM-change and port-out protection.** [@fcc-simswap-2023]
- [ ] **Turn on your exchange's withdrawal allowlist** with the longest waiting period you can live with. [@binance-whitelist]
- [ ] **Use unique passwords from a password manager,** and an email address used only for financial accounts.

## Signing and sending {#free-signing}

- [ ] **Read the hardware wallet's screen before you approve.** Never approve data it can't decode. [@certik-bybit-2025]
- [ ] **Review token approvals monthly** and revoke what you don't use. Approve only what you're about to spend. [@ms-ice-phishing-2022; @metamask-revoke]
- [ ] **Keep a vault, a spending wallet and a burner.** Never connect the vault to a new site.
- [ ] **Send from your address book, never your history,** and check the whole address. Lookalike addresses hide in your history. [@cmu-address-poisoning]

## Links, apps and downloads {#free-links}

- [ ] **Arrive through bookmarks,** never search ads or links in messages. [@ic3-search-ads-2022]
- [ ] **Install wallets and extensions only from the maker's own site,** in a browser profile used only for crypto. [@koi-foxywallet-2025]
- [ ] **Never run a stranger's code,** a "meeting app" they send, or a command a web page tells you to paste. [@ic3-dprk-2024; @ms-clickfix-2025]
- [ ] **Keep your phone, computer and browser updated,** and remove apps and extensions you no longer use.

## People, scams and AI {#free-people}

- [ ] **Agree a family code word** for urgent money calls, and call back on a number you know. [@ic3-genai-2024; @ftc-family-emergency]
- [ ] **Walk away from guaranteed returns,** "safe wallet" transfers and fees to withdraw. They're always scams. [@ftc-crypto-scams]
- [ ] **Ignore anyone offering to recover stolen crypto,** including fake law firms and fake FBI agents. [@ic3-recovery-2024; @ic3-impersonation-2026]

:::steps If something goes wrong
1. Stop signing and close the tab. If you ran something, disconnect that device.
2. From a clean device, move funds from anything exposed into a new wallet. Change your email password and 2FA.
3. Report it: ic3.gov and ReportFraud.ftc.gov in the US, or your national cybercrime service. Keep the transaction IDs.
:::

@columnbreak

:::redflag Badger's Red Flags: hear one of these, it's a scam
- "Move your funds to this safe wallet."
- "Your wallet needs to be validated or synced."
- "Guaranteed returns." "We'll double it."
- "Pay a fee or tax to withdraw your profits."
- "We can recover your stolen crypto."
- "Read me the code we just sent you."
:::

:::fix
If you do only one thing today: find and delete every digital copy of your seed phrase. If one existed, move your funds to a new wallet.
:::
