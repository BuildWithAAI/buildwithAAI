## Rules for our channel

1. **Genre, not copy.** Second-person life-simulation storytelling is a genre we can work in. Mr. Ranks' character, art, scripts, thumbnails and title list are not ours to reuse.
   - Our host is a new original character.
   - Our art is code-drawn vector, not his painted comic style.
   - Every script is written from our own research.
2. **Inauthentic-content risk.** His catalogue is close to the pattern YouTube names: "templated storylines… minimal variation". Every one of our videos needs a different story structure, real researched facts, and a point of view. We do not swap a noun into the same script.
3. **No income claims.** "Crypto millionaire"-type stories are fiction. We label them as a story and never imply viewers will earn money. We prefer the realistic or cautionary angle (taxes, security, scams, rug pulls). That angle is also the AAI project's founding reason.
4. **Length.** His episodes run 25–30 min. Our pipeline pilot is 60–90 s by brief. Full episodes are a later decision once render time per minute is measured.
