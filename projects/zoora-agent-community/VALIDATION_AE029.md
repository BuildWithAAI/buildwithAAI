## Corrections and limits

The documented absence of queue backup/restore is addressed through a coordinated package and persistent queue recovery mode, rather than reopening spending from copied state. Missing provider history after any queue dispatch fence now blocks normal execution before the worker can recreate a journal. Provider claim timestamps are sampled after the committed local fence. An early lease rehearsal incorrectly tried to run at a time preceding its later restore; the existing clock-regression guard correctly refused it. The harness was corrected to restore at the intended injected lease time; no clock check was relaxed.

Service becomes **0.11.0**, payment queue **2**. Community schema stays **6** and provider journal **3**. Existing queue schema 1 requires an exact verified backup migration; restores always become recovery-only, including legacy snapshots. Partial publication may leave an incomplete quarantined directory; operators inspect it and restore into a new destination. No overwrite or reactivation command exists.

**External Bridge requests: zero.** Provider account/route acceptance remains unverified. There is no Phantom signer, real-asset authority, card rail, live payment, fee/funds verification, delivery or verified settlement. The source service is a separate store and must be backed up/recovered separately. Raw administrative copies, hostile trusted-host changes, external wallet activity, Windows ACLs and physical power-loss guarantees remain outside this verification.
