## Findings today, most important first

1. **Nothing has run on the owner's computer.** The bridge has met only a stand-in for MetaTrader. No run has used a model key, so her own designs and her own words have only been tested with made-up model answers. No real graphics card has drawn her world. No real wallet has bought a home. All four wait for setup day, by the owner's direction (`docs/SETUP_DAY.md`).

2. **With no working model, the engine builds for her.** On a fresh run with no model key she laid land within 40 seconds. Without a model, or when her own design is refused, the engine picks one of twelve things it knows how to make, by her mood, and puts it at a place drawn by chance, up to 24 a day. `docs/FORGE.md` says so. The owner's rule is that she builds her world herself and nothing is built for her. This is his to decide: keep these as her habits, or switch them off so that no model means no build and a refused design means she tries again. Until then, the model key has to be set before the engine is first started.

3. **Nothing decides to place an order.** Her rules, the bridge and the price history are built, and no part of her reaches them. Backlog 0v.

4. **She trades no coin.** No signer exists that speaks the engine's protocol, so a coin order has nowhere to go. The signer draft in PR #92 is a dry run and has its own format. When one exists, a coin result will still be the quote's figure until the signer reports the fill. Backlog 0t.

5. **A visitor cannot load her world on the public site.** See public mode above. Backlog 0c.

6. **Her world stops at 400 builds.** Past that her building step is refused (`FORGE_FULL`). The placement index is ready for a million. The listing and the live view are not. Backlog 0c.

7. **The nine open findings of the security audit of 2026-11-20 still stand** (`docs/SECURITY_AUDIT_2026-11-20.md`, 14 to 22). Four matter before real keys go in: the model key is sent to whatever address is set, plain http included (18); the desk proxy listens on every network interface (15); the owner token and the Jarvis token have no minimum length (17); and with no caps set a purchase the owner confirms has no ceiling in code (16), which the owner's rule, her balance as the only ceiling, is meant to replace (0e).

8. **Five dependency updates for the engine are open and each fails CI as it stands:** Next 16, TypeScript 7, `@types/node` 26, Playwright 1.65 and suncalc 2 (PRs #119 to #123). Two were read: suncalc 2 breaks the sky tests and Next 16 breaks the production build. None is a security fix, and none should be merged as it is.

9. **Two lines in the repository said money was test only.** The first paragraph of `README.md` and the package description. Fixed in this change.

10. **Small things.** On a wide screen the AI notice covers the first line of the look-around tip until it is dismissed. The build log page carries its dated history, so it still has this morning's entries that say paper.

Not checked: the public site at zoora.ai. It could not be reached from the build machine. Nothing in this work deployed anything.
