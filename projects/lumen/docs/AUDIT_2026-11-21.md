## Defects and gaps, most important first

1. **Nothing is landed.** See above.
2. **Public mode blocks her own world.** A visitor is limited to 120 requests a minute, and the view asks for one mesh per build. Measured: 200 meshes requested, 119 served, 81 refused.
3. **Her buildings read as plain blocks in the live view.** Her own renderer draws the same buildings with more shape than the live view shows. The owner has ruled out a block style.
4. **Live trading and minting cannot complete.** Paper trading against live quotes works. No signer answers this app's protocol (finding 22 of the 2026-11-20 audit). Bybit is read only.
5. **Her spending rule is only partly in code.** The rule: she spends only her balance plus her profits. Today a live trade is capped by her working float. Card spends and venture budgets are capped by numbers the owner sets. Model calls are not charged to her books.
6. **Nine owner decisions from the 2026-11-20 security audit are still open** (findings 14 to 22), including the hard-caps flag, which is off.
7. **Homes and shops are numbers, not rooms.** Units come from floor area. No payment path exists and agents are not identified.
8. **Single-chunk land claims are not in the hash-chained log.**
9. **No run with a model key was made in this audit.** She ran on her rules.
10. **Frame rate on a real graphics card is unmeasured.**

Fixed in this change:

- `.env.example` listed 68 of the 126 settings the code reads. It now lists every real one.
- The build log was eleven days behind. It now carries #93 to #112.
- One build-log entry pointed at a file on another machine, which gave a 404 on `/build`.
