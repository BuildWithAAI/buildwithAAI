03 / REPRODUCE

Assumptions stay visible.

The reference campaign has no fee. Any fee routing is an illustrative sensitivity experiment, not an approved AAI policy. Burn and liquidity shares are rounded down per payment; the treasury receives the remainder. Fees are not returned when a provider voluntarily returns a payment.

Providers, ordinary clients and attacker accounts have fixed roles. Clients do not earn new income in this model. Payment observations are immediate synthetic fixtures. The operator cap limits outstanding unpaid reservations under declared identities; it cannot establish common control or prevent hidden Sybils. The recovery phase is observed without automatic retries or rerouting.
