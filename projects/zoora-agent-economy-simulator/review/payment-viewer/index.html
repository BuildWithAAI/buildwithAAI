03 / INTERPRET AND REPRODUCE

Timing rules stay visible.

RESERVE ON QUOTE holds a work slot before payment. STRICT OBSERVATION reserves no slot until a backed payment is observed before the funding cutoff. EVIDENCE GRACE checks the original simulated transfer time and allows bounded observation lag. Funded queues use receipt validation order; they do not prioritize known attack labels.

The grace period cannot make an actually late transfer timely. All known transfers remain in the synthetic wallet ledger, including overpayments rejected by the agreement adapter. A missed window, rejection or dispute never reverses funds. Voluntary returns are separate transfer facts.

Nonpaying identities cannot occupy funded work slots, but paid spam can. Quote metadata, queue fairness, common control, delayed observations and failed work still have limits. This model does not authenticate real wallets, forecast adoption, or establish economic stability.
