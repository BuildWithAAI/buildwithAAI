03 / INTERPRET AND REPRODUCE

A wallet limit is not identity.

EVIDENCE HOLD is the unmodified capacity baseline. PENDING QUOTA allows at most one unpaid held permission per wallet/provider pair. QUOTA COOLDOWN adds a waiting period after an unused permission expires. Paid permissions do not create an unpaid-expiry cooldown. Rule checks never inspect the fixture's attack label.

Concentrated unpaid requests use one declared attack wallet. Rotating unpaid requests use the full bounded attack-wallet pool and repeatedly renew permissions. Slow ordinary clients miss their first send cutoff and retry; a cooldown can obstruct them too. A quota can throttle legitimate bursts. The experiment neither detects common control nor solves adaptive Sybil abuse.

All policies keep the full observation-window capacity hold and check client permission before a simulated debit. Evidence outside the promised window and provider failures still leave exposure. A refusal or cooldown does not freeze a wallet, seize funds or refund a transfer. Started/acknowledged outcomes are scripted, not independent work-quality judgments.

Exports require native replay. This unsigned HTML has basic display checks; rerun the CLI for full verification. Desktop/390px Chromium checks do not establish actual iPhone/Safari operation.
