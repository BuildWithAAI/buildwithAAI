03 / INTERPRET AND REPRODUCE

A reservation has a cost.

UNRESERVED PAYMENT grants permission without holding a slot. FUNDING WINDOW HOLD releases an unstarted slot at the send cutoff. EVIDENCE WINDOW HOLD keeps that slot until the observation cutoff. All policies bind amount, payer, provider, agreement and delivery deadline, require the same send/evidence windows, and refuse new permission when its worst permitted observation leaves too little service time.

The simulated client checks permission before debiting its wallet. Each token can be consumed once. Repeated unused authorizations can starve legitimate work; paying attackers and provider failure remain harmful. Evidence arriving outside the promised window can leave a real synthetic transfer without a start. A refusal or expiry never refunds a payment. Voluntary returns remain separate facts.

These results assume an honest local capacity book and compliant fixture clients. Real providers may oversell or go offline; other payment paths may bypass the guard. No production authorization service, signed capability or useful-work verifier is implemented. Results are not an adoption forecast or evidence of economic stability. Actual iPhone/Safari execution remains unverified.
