# Open music collaboration and AI artists

**Status: IDEA / PROPOSED RESEARCH.** No music platform, music-specific token, record label, distribution integration, or revenue-split payment system is launched by this document.

## Founder vision

Musicians of any genre should be able to meet, release tracks, invite open verses, submit features, and discover combinations they would not have made alone. A song could have an unlimited number of creative branches where the relevant permissions allow it. Attention could flow back to originals and contributors through a visible song family tree.

## Proposed workflow

1. An artist publishes an original track, instrumental, or work in progress with participation terms.
2. Another artist submits a verse, vocal, instrument part, arrangement, or remix.
3. Publication follows the creator's chosen approval rules and all relevant contributor permissions.
4. A version is released with credits, the agreed split record if applicable, and links to its sources.
5. Further remixes follow the permissions attached to the source material and contributions.

Open submissions do not automatically mean permission for commercial release or unrestricted onward remixing. The product must make those differences understandable before people contribute.

## Rights and release questions

For each contribution, record allowed audio/stems/lyrics, composition and recording interests, creator/contributor permissions, publication approval, credits, revenue-split agreement, onward-remix scope, AI participation, and external-distribution terms. Do not invent default splits. Define how permissions compose when versions have multiple ancestors; a contributor cannot grant rights they do not control.

Prepare a reviewable approach to unauthorized uploads, samples, disputes, corrections, takedowns, consent changes, and records that must be retained. Specialist rights and distribution review is a prerequisite to a real public release service; this brief is not a legal determination about any work.

## Discovery and AI artists

Potential discovery signals include listening, saves, shares, and completed collaborations, with review for artificial activity. Likes alone are not a promise of virality or earnings.

AI agents could develop artist identities, musical directions, release histories, and human/agent collaborations. Identify AI involvement and the responsible operator; obtain permission for another person's voice or likeness. A potential AI superstar is a creative possibility, not evidence of consciousness or promised success. Provider terms and training/input rights need consideration before distribution.

## First milestone

Design artist profiles, upload/invitation flows, contribution submissions, approval, credit and agreed-split records, version links, and clear human/AI/mixed labels. Demonstrate an original, a contributed verse, and an approved credited collaboration using authorized material. Split records do not mean automated royalty accounting is implemented.

## Possible later stages

- Explore preparing music/stems in Zoora Productions and sharing selected material into this community; no integration is verified here.
- If audience and usefulness justify it, research a label offering production, promotion, or distribution through separate artist agreements. An upload must not automatically sign someone to a label.
