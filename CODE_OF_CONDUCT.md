# Community code of conduct

**Proposed policy.** Be creative with the memes and careful with people's trust.

Welcome people across experience levels. Explain disagreements, critique ideas rather than people, credit sources, and report negative findings honestly. Recognition should reflect useful work rather than holdings or price promotion.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, impersonation, spam, scams, fake results, undisclosed promotion, and requests for wallet secrets are unacceptable. Do not pressure members to buy or share credentials.

Report only non-sensitive conduct concerns publicly. For personal information, ask the maintainer for a private route without posting details. The current maintainer is `zooratechnologies`; no dedicated reporting address is established here.

Maintainers may request edits, remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation. Record reasons where appropriate without exposing private information. Affected people may request review; an independent appeal body is not established. Publish material policy changes for review.
