## Changes

Use a focused PR with full destination, purpose, publishable control evidence, verifier, timestamp, and explicit owner approval. Avoid shorteners and lookalike domains. Compare exact mint addresses and chain evidence; a ticker, image, or suffix is insufficient.

Record reasons for replacements and preserve history. Any link enabling wallet interaction is sensitive. A repository can also be compromised: inspect history and decisions when information conflicts. No file alone guarantees safety.
